Child Safety Standards

Our Commitment to Protection

At Bliitz, the safety of children is our highest priority. We are committed to maintaining a secure, monitored, and compliant platform that protects young users from harm.

🛡️ Reporting Abuse

Users can report child safety concerns directly in the Bliitz app using the Report Section. All reports are taken seriously and reviewed promptly by our dedicated safety team to ensure swift action against any violations.

💬 Active Support

Bliitz has an active support team available 24/7 to respond to any concerns or questions regarding child safety. Our team is trained to handle sensitive reports with care and urgency. You can contact us anytime via tech.bliitzapp@gmail.com.

👁️ Moderation and Safety

Bliitz actively monitors and moderates all content using both automated systems and human review. Any content that violates child safety policies is immediately removed, and accounts that fail to comply are permanently terminated to ensure a safe environment for all users.

⚖️ Compliance

Bliitz complies with all relevant child protection laws including COPPA and GDPR. We work closely with regional and national authorities, reporting violations as required by law. We are committed to creating a safe, trusted community for all users.

ZERO TOLERANCE FOR CHILD EXPLOITATION
