Bliitz Child Safety Standards

Reporting Abuse

Users can report child safety concerns directly in the Bliitz app using the Report Section. All reports are taken seriously and reviewed promptly.

Active Support

Bliitz has an active support team available to respond to any concerns or questions regarding child safety. You can contact us anytime via tech.bliitzapp@gmail.com.

Moderation and Safety

Bliitz actively monitors and moderates content. Any content that violates child safety policies is automatically banned, and accounts that fail to comply are terminated to ensure a safe environment.

Compliance

Bliitz complies with all relevant child protection laws and reports to regional or national authorities as required. We are committed to creating a safe community for all users.
